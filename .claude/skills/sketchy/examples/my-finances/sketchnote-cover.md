# Brief: sketchnote "cover" style (the garden-skills README covers)

Templates combined: `references/infographics/hand-drawn-infographic.md` + a hub-and-spoke panel layout (a central title window, 6–9 numbered panels around it, a strip of icon tiles).
Part A is the reusable fill-in version and part B is filled in for My finances. Built in code as: a hub grid page (see code/translate.md → "hub with panels around it"). Not built in the app yet.

---

## A. Reusable template (fill the {braces})

```
A hand-drawn sketchnote infographic cover, 16:9 landscape, in the style of a neat bullet-journal page drawn with
fine black ink pen and soft coloured pencils / light watercolour washes.

Background: warm cream paper (#f4efe2) with subtle paper grain. No pure white, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

Centre (the hub): a large hand-drawn browser window with rounded corners, three small dots and a little toolbar at the top.
Inside it, the title "{TITLE}" in big, bold, black hand-lettered marker capitals, perfectly spelled.
{Optional: a short subtitle under it in small neat handwriting: "{SUBTITLE}"}

Around the hub: {8} hand-drawn rounded rectangle panels in a ring (3 above, 1–2 on each side, 3 below), connected to the
hub by thin curvy pencil arrows. Each panel has:
- a small number badge in a hand-drawn circle at its top-left corner (1 to {8}, clockwise from top-left)
- a short title in neat handwritten print
- one tiny sketch illustration (simple, cute, 2–3 strokes of detail, lightly coloured)
- two or three short checkmark lines in small handwriting
Panels:
1. "{P1 title}" — sketch: {P1 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
2. "{P2 title}" — sketch: {P2 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
3. "{P3 title}" — sketch: {P3 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
4. "{P4 title}" — sketch: {P4 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
5. "{P5 title}" — sketch: {P5 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
6. "{P6 title}" — sketch: {P6 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
7. "{P7 title}" — sketch: {P7 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"
8. "{P8 title}" — sketch: {P8 drawing} — checks: "{a}", "{b}"

Under the hub: a row of {5} small square icon tiles, each a simple doodle icon with a one- or two-word caption:
"{icon 1}", "{icon 2}", "{icon 3}", "{icon 4}", "{icon 5}".

Colour: black ink outlines for everything; panel fills are pale washes that rotate through {palette, e.g. sage green,
dusty blue, soft peach, muted lavender, mustard}; colour stays inside the lines with a tiny gap, like hand colouring.

Decorations, sprinkled lightly: small sparkles and four-point stars, a few tiny hearts, dotted lines, little leafy

Style rules: every line has a gentle hand-drawn wobble; all text is hand-lettered (no computer fonts); text is short,
large enough to read and spelled exactly as given; no gibberish or filler text anywhere; generous whitespace;
clear hierarchy: title > panel titles > checks > doodles.
```

## B. Filled in for My finances (decorative cover, no figures)

```
A hand-drawn sketchnote infographic cover, 16:9 landscape, in the style of a neat bullet-journal page drawn with
fine black ink pen and soft coloured pencils / light watercolour washes.

Background: warm cream paper (#f3eedf) with subtle paper grain. No pure white, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

Centre (the hub): a large hand-drawn browser window with rounded corners, three small dots and a little toolbar at the top.
Inside it, the title "My Finances" in big, bold, black hand-lettered marker capitals, perfectly spelled.
Under it, in small neat handwriting: "a private notebook for money and taxes".

Around the hub: 8 hand-drawn rounded rectangle panels in a ring (3 above, 1 on each side, 3 below), connected to the hub
by thin curvy pencil arrows. Each panel has a small number badge in a hand-drawn circle at its top-left corner, a short
title in neat handwritten print, one tiny cute sketch, and two short checkmark lines in small handwriting:
1. "Your worth" — sketch: a gently rising line chart with a little star at the top — checks: "best month", "at this pace"
2. "Day by day" — sketch: a small calendar grid with pencil-hatched squares and a "PAY" stamp — checks: "big days", "no-spend days"
3. "Where it went" — sketch: a smiling wallet tossing coins toward little jars — checks: "jars", "cartoon cards"
4. "Debt-free road" — sketch: a winding road ending at a little flag — checks: "highest rate first", "finish line"
5. "Statements" — sketch: a bank statement under a magnifying glass — checks: "every transaction", "sorted for you"
6. "Taxes" — sketch: a tax form, a small coin and a calendar page — checks: "five returns", "CPA report"
7. "Advisor's review" — sketch: three taped sticky notes and a smiley face — checks: "do these next", "health check"
8. "Private & offline" — sketch: a laptop with a padlock and a crossed-out cloud — checks: "stays on this computer", "locked backups"

Under the hub: a row of 5 small square icon tiles, each a simple doodle icon with a short caption:
"Offline", "Lock", "Backups", "Light & dark", "Notebook style".

Colour: black ink outlines for everything; panel fills are pale washes that rotate through deep teal (#1b4b5a, very light),
sienna (#a04a2a, very light), sage green (#2f6b3f, very light), ochre (#a8812a, very light) and plum (#8a4d6e, very light);
colour stays inside the lines with a tiny gap, like hand colouring. Use teal as the one accent for emphasis.

Decorations, sprinkled lightly: small sparkles and four-point stars, a few tiny hearts, a few falling coins, dotted
lines, little leafy sprigs along the bottom edge.

Style rules: every line has a gentle hand-drawn wobble; all text is hand-lettered (no computer fonts); text is short,
large enough to read and spelled exactly as given; no numbers, amounts or percentages anywhere; no gibberish or filler
text; generous whitespace; clear hierarchy: title > panel titles > checks > doodles.
```

Use with sketchy: "use sketchy to make a cover like this for <site/part>". The skill fills part A from the site and builds it as an HTML page.
