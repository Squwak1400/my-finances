# Brief: Where your money went, cute illustrated infographic

Template: `references/infographics/hand-drawn-infographic.md` (structured natural language), palette mapped to the app's Ledger tokens.
Built in code as: the "Illustrated" view in Statements → Where your money went (`Redesign build/notebook.js` → renderPic, CSS `#nbFlow .ill*` in `notebook.css`).
The brief asks for blank amount tags; in code those tags hold the live figures. Wallet mascot → SVG with face(); cards → cartoon ART objects; doodles → SVG sparkles and coins; hover light-up added per code/translate.md.

---

A hand-drawn educational infographic in the style of a high-quality bullet journal page.

Topic: where one month's take-home pay goes, told as a friendly little journey from a pay cheque to the places it is spent.

Aspect ratio: 4:3 landscape.

Background:
- Warm cream paper #f3eedf with subtle grain, the faintest dotted grid.
- One or two short strips of washi tape in the corners, washed teal #1b4b5a and ochre #a8812a at low opacity.
- DO NOT use pure white or pure black background.

Color palette (limit to these, used sparingly; fills are light washes of each colour, outlines are ink):
- Ink #1a1a18 for every outline and all lettering
- Ledger teal #1b4b5a (main accent), sienna #a04a2a, green #2f6b3f, ochre #a8812a, plum #8a4d6e, indigo #4b4f7c
- Paper shade #ebe4d0 for card backs

Title:
- "Where your money went" in large hand-lettered print at the top, inside a hand-drawn wavy banner.
- Subtitle below in smaller handwritten print: "one month, one pay cheque".

Narrator:
- A single cute mascot at the top centre: a smiling wallet character with dot eyes, rosy cheeks and little arms, tossing coins. Curvy dotted arrows run from it down to every card.

Body: 8 hand-drawn cards in a loose 4 × 2 grid, each a slightly different rounded or wobbly shape, each with:
- a cute cartoon object with a simple face (dot eyes, small smile, rosy cheeks), 1–2 strokes of detail, NOT realistic:
  1. Housing — a little house with a smiling door
  2. Groceries — a paper grocery bag with a carrot and leaves peeking out
  3. Dining out — a steaming noodle bowl
  4. Transportation — a small round car with headlight eyes
  5. Shopping — a shopping bag with a heart on it
  6. Utilities — a glowing light bulb
  7. Debt payments — a credit card with a nervous sweat drop
  8. Saved — a happy piggy bank with a coin going in
- the category name in bold handwritten text
- an empty hand-drawn tag where an amount would go (leave it BLANK, no numbers)

Doodle decorations (sprinkle naturally, not symmetrically):
- tiny stars, sparkles, small hearts, curvy arrows, dotted lines, a few falling coins
- hand-drawn underline under the title

Style enforcement:
- ALL lines have visible hand-drawn wobble, never perfectly straight
- ALL colour fills leave a tiny gap before the outline (hand-painted feel)
- All text is hand-lettered, NO computer fonts
- No gradients, no shadows, no glass or metal effects
- NO numbers, digits, currency amounts or percentages anywhere in the image

Composition:
- Generous whitespace (30–40% of the canvas)
- Hierarchy: title > mascot > cards > doodles
- Cards large enough to read; do not cram

Language: English.
