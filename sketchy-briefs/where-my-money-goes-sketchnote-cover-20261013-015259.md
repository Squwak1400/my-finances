# Brief: Where my money goes, sketchnote cover (static)

Template: `examples/my-finances/sketchnote-cover.md` part A (hand-drawn-infographic + hub-and-spoke), palette mapped to the app's Ledger tokens.
Built in code as: `Where my money goes (sketchnote cover).html` in the project root, a standalone static page drawn from one `DATA` array (sample numbers). Same categories, groups and colours as `Where my money goes (sketchy).html`, so either can become the interactive version.

---

A hand-drawn sketchnote infographic cover, 16:9 landscape, neat bullet-journal page in fine ink with light washes.

Background: warm cream paper {--surface}, faint dotted grid. No pure white, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

Centre (the hub): a large hand-drawn browser window (rounded corners, three dots, a little address bar). Inside it the title "Where my money goes" in big hand lettering, the subtitle "{take-home pay} take-home · one month", and one hand-hatched dollar bar split into every category in its group colour, with a small smiling wallet tossing coins.

Around the hub: 8 hand-drawn panels in a ring (3 above, 1 each side, 3 below), joined to the hub by thin curvy dotted arrows. Each panel: a number badge (1–8, clockwise from top-left, biggest first), the category name in handwriting, a cute cartoon with a face, the amount {amount} in mono, and two checkmark lines: "{share}% of pay" and a short note.
1. Housing (house) "rent + insurance"  2. Saved (piggy bank) "TFSA + emergency fund"  3. Groceries (grocery bag) "about $155 a week"
4. Transportation (car) "gas + transit pass"  5. Debt payments (worried credit card) "credit card first"
6. Dining out (noodle bowl) "about 8 meals out"  7. Shopping (heart bag) "clothes + home bits"  8. Utilities (light bulb) "hydro + internet"

Under the hub: a row of 5 small icon tiles: "Spent {spent}", "Saved {saved}", "Debt paid {debt}", "Biggest: {top category}", "Every dollar has a job".

Colour (one per group; fills are pale washes, outlines ink {--text}, teal is the accent): Home {--s1}, Everyday {--s4}, Fun {--s2}, Debt {--s5}, Savings {--s3}.

Decorations, sprinkled lightly: sparkles and four-point stars, tiny hearts, falling coins, leafy sprigs along the bottom edge.

Hierarchy: title > panel titles > amounts > checks > doodles. Generous whitespace.
