How to export your history

Always pick the widest date range, starting before your first purchase. Menu names move around between app versions.

Crypto.com app
  1. Open Accounts, then Crypto Wallet (or Transaction History).
  2. Tap the export or document icon and choose Export to CSV.
  3. Pick the crypto transactions export and the full date range. It arrives by email or download.
Kraken
  1. Sign in on kraken.com and open Documents (or History), then Export.
  2. Choose Ledgers (not Trades), all fields, the full date range.
  3. Download the zip and unzip it; import ledgers.csv.

Where these go on the return

      
For discussion with your CPA, who confirms the final treatment.

      

        
Capital gains (Schedule 3)Report the proceeds and adjusted cost base of crypto disposals on Schedule 3. The taxable half flows to line 12700.

        
Crypto incomeStaking, interest and rewards are usually other income (line 13000). If CRA would see your activity as a business, all of it is business income instead.

        
Business or capital?Frequent trading, short holding periods and running it like a business point to business income: 100% taxable, but losses are fully deductible.

    What usually applies to travelling and seasonal jobs
    
      
    General CRA rules. Your employer's setup decides which ones you can use.
    
      
    
        
    Per diemsA reasonable travel allowance for work away from your home base is usually tax-free and stays off your T4. If your employer put it in box 14, it was taxed and you may be able to claim meals instead. You can't have both for the same meals.
    
        
    Transport employee meals and lodgingForm TL2, signed by your employer, lets transport employees claim meals by the simplified method ($17 a meal, up to $51 a day before 2023; $23 and $69 from 2023; one meal per 4 hours away; 50% deductible) and lodging they paid on trips of 12 hours or more away from base. Ask each employer whether it counts as a transport employer and will sign a TL2.
    
        
    Other employment expensesTransport employee meals use the employer-signed TL2. Other work costs on line 22900, including travel meals for other employees, need a signed T2200. Licence renewals, medicals, tools and uniforms usually aren't deductible for employees.
    
        
    Union and professional duesDues on your T4 (box 44) or receipts go on line 21200.
    
        
    TrainingTraining at a certified school can give a T2202 for the tuition credit (line 32300), and the Canada Training Credit from 2020 on.
    
        
    Camps and remote basesBoard and lodging at a special or remote work site your employer provides is usually tax-free. Northern residents deduction (line 25500) only applies if you lived in a prescribed zone for 6 months or more.
    
        
    Two employers in one yearEach employer takes off CPP and EI from zero, so a job change partway through a year can mean you paid too much. CRA refunds the extra when you file (lines 44800 and 45000).
    
        
    Seasonal EIOff-season EI (T4E) is taxable and often has little tax taken off, so it can leave a balance owing.
    
        
    ContractingIf you worked as a self-employed contractor, licences, medicals, training, gear, travel and 50% of meals are business expenses on form T2125, and you pay both halves of CPP.

    How the numbers are worked out
    
        
    The CRA rules this page applies.
    
        
    
          
    Average cost (ACB)All units of a coin are one pool across every account. Each purchase adds its cost; each sale takes out the average cost of what was sold.
    
          
    What's taxableSelling for dollars, swapping coins, spending crypto, and crypto you earn. Moving coins between your own accounts isn't.
    
          
    Superficial lossesIf you bought the same coin within 30 days before or after a loss and still held it 30 days later, that part of the loss is denied and added to your cost instead.
    
          
    Losses carry forwardA net capital loss can be applied against later capital gains, or carried back three years.
    
          
    Late filingPenalty is 5% of the balance owing plus 1% per month (up to 12), plus interest, only on years you owe.
